Approach

Investigation methodology

01 · Acquire Collect evidence using appropriate forensic procedures.
02 · Verify Confirm evidence integrity and document its provenance.
03 · Examine Identify relevant logs, files and system artefacts.
04 · Correlate Compare multiple evidence sources and assess context.
05 · Report Present supported findings, limitations and conclusions.
